# Initial Public Web Observations

**Checked:** 2026-11-01  
**Scope:** Public web search snippets and local business search results. This is not a full crawl, Search Console audit, or verified performance report.

## Confirmed public-facing themes

The public homepage presents Jay's Trax Rentals as an equipment and trailer rental business in Saskatoon. It describes categories including skid steers, mini excavators, dump trailers, enclosed cargo trailers, equipment trailers, and landscaping tools, with daily/weekly/monthly rentals mentioned. The site also mentions delivery to surrounding areas including Warman and Martensville.

Search results surfaced both:
- `/equipment-rental-saskatoon`

## Opportunities to investigate

1. **Equipment-specific landing pages:** confirm that key rental types have useful, indexable pages with verified specifications, rates, attachments, and booking links.
2. **Booking clarity:** test how quickly a user can check availability, understand the total cost, and book on mobile.
3. **Answer-ready content:** add real answers about towing, delivery, rental periods, deposits, attachments, and choosing equipment.
4. **Local trust:** verify Google Business Profile data, genuine reviews, photos, and consistent business contact information.
5. **AEO monitoring:** use a fixed set of prompts in answer engines and record citations and accuracy over time.
6. **Conversion measurement:** check whether booking starts, completed bookings, phone clicks, and inquiry submissions are measured reliably.
