# Jayden 2026 — Jay's Trax Rentals SEO & Sales Growth

**Website:** https://www.jaystraxrentals.com/  
**Market:** Saskatoon, Saskatchewan and verified surrounding service areas  
**Project focus:** Local SEO, organic rental inquiries, booking conversion, and B2B lead generation.

## Goals

1. Improve visibility for high-intent equipment and trailer rental searches.
2. Make important rental pages easier to discover, understand, and rank.
3. Increase qualified booking requests and phone/email inquiries.
4. Strengthen accurate local business listings and review signals.
5. Build a repeatable sales prospecting process for contractors and local businesses.
6. Track outcomes so work is prioritized by leads and revenue, not vanity metrics.

## Workstreams

- **SEO baseline:** indexation, crawlability, titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, canonical tags, structured data, mobile experience, and page speed.
- **Keyword and landing-page map:** match search intent to existing equipment, trailer, and category pages; identify genuine content gaps.
- **Local SEO:** Google Business Profile, NAP consistency, reviews, legitimate citations, service-area accuracy, and local competitor review.
- **Conversion optimization:** booking path, calls to action, rental details, availability/rates clarity, and analytics events.
- **Sales pipeline:** identify suitable local contractors and businesses, qualify fit, draft personalized outreach, and track outcomes.
- **Reporting:** monthly baseline comparisons for impressions, clicks, rankings, inquiries, booking starts, completed bookings, and qualified sales leads where data is available.

## Operating rules

- Do not make live website, DNS, analytics, listing, or advertising changes without explicit authorization.
- Verify current website content and actual service coverage before recommending location-specific claims.
- Do not create fake reviews, mass-submit low-quality citations, buy manipulative links, or send bulk unsolicited outreach.
- Preserve existing URLs where possible; document redirects and risks before any URL changes.
- Treat rankings and traffic as indicators. Prioritize qualified inquiries, completed bookings, and revenue.
- Record the date, evidence, priority, recommended action, owner, and status for each finding.
- Do not report a task as completed until it has been verified.
