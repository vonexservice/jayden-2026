# Answer-First Content Briefs

These are content briefs, not approved final copy. Confirm all operational facts with the business before publishing.

## Brief 1: Mini excavator rental in Saskatoon

**Search intent:** Find, compare, and book a mini excavator for a specific job.

**Primary page:** The strongest existing mini excavator rental page.

**Questions the page should answer:**
- What mini excavator models are currently available?
- What are their operating weight, digging depth, width, and access requirements?
- What jobs are they suited for, and what jobs are they not suited for?
- Which bucket or attachments are included?
- What are the daily, weekly, and monthly rates?
- Is delivery available to the customer's location and what does it cost?
- What are the pickup, return, deposit, and cancellation terms?
- How does a customer check availability?

**Recommended structure:**
1. Direct opening answer that names the rental, market, and next step.
2. Available models and specifications.
3. Common uses and access considerations.
4. Rate and rental-period details.
5. Delivery/pickup information.
6. Requirements and policies.
7. Original photos and booking CTA.
8. Related attachments and FAQs.

## Brief 2: Mini skid steer / Bobcat MT100 rental

**Search intent:** Find a compact loader for a narrow-access landscaping or property project.

Include only verified specifications and attachments. Explain typical applications such as moving soil/material, landscaping, and work in restricted spaces where appropriate. State operating dimensions, attachment compatibility, transport needs, rates, and delivery only after verification.

## Brief 3: Dump trailer rental

**Search intent:** Choose a trailer for hauling debris, soil, gravel, or renovation waste.

Answer:
- What trailer sizes and configurations are available?
- What is the payload/capacity and what are the loading restrictions?
- What tow vehicle, hitch, and brake setup are required?
- Are there restrictions on material types or disposal?
- Is delivery available?
- What rental periods, fees, deposits, and return requirements apply?

Do not conflate trailer volume with legal payload. Verify actual specifications and safe towing requirements.

## Brief 4: Equipment delivery and service area

**Search intent:** Know whether the business can deliver a particular rental to a particular address/community.

List only confirmed communities and explain how delivery eligibility, fees, scheduling, and access are determined. Include Saskatoon, Warman, and Martensville only if the current operating model supports those claims. Avoid generating separate low-value doorway pages for every nearby town.
