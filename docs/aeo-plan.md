# AEO Plan — Answer Engine Optimization

**Goal:** Make Jay's Trax Rentals easy for search engines and answer engines to understand, cite, and recommend for relevant local equipment and trailer rental questions.

**Important:** No AEO tactic guarantees inclusion in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, or another answer engine. The plan prioritizes discoverable pages, accurate facts, useful original content, and clear entity information.

## What official guidance means in practice

Google's guidance says the existing SEO foundations apply to AI search features: pages must be eligible for search, important content should be crawlable and available as text, internal links should make it discoverable, structured data must match visible content, and business profiles should be kept accurate. There is no special Google AI schema requirement. Avoid relying on invented "AI ranking hacks" or unnecessary machine-readable files.

Reference: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features  
People-first content guidance: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

## Priority 1 — Establish facts and access

- [ ] Confirm actual business name, service area, pickup arrangements, public phone, contact methods, hours, and current booking process.
- [ ] Confirm the active fleet, attachments, sizes/specifications, rental rates, deposits, delivery fees, minimum rental periods, towing requirements, and restrictions.
- [ ] Get Search Console, analytics, booking platform, and Google Business Profile access where authorized.
- [ ] Record the baseline of branded and non-branded visibility.
- [ ] Test whether important pages can be crawled, indexed, and rendered as useful text.

Do not publish any price, inventory, service-area, or availability claim until verified with the business.

## Priority 2 — Answer high-intent questions on the right pages

Create or improve content where it directly helps a prospective renter make a decision. Prefer adding answers to relevant existing equipment pages over producing many thin blog posts.

### Equipment detail page information

For each important rental, include accurate and easy-to-find:
- What the equipment is suited for and common use cases
- Key dimensions, operating capacity, digging depth, payload, or towing specs where applicable
- Included attachments and what costs extra
- Daily/weekly/monthly rate and what the rate includes, if confirmed
- Pickup and delivery options, service-area limitations, and delivery fees
- Booking steps, deposits, damage/cleaning/fuel terms, and cancellation rules
- Safety, operator, tow vehicle, hitch, and license requirements where applicable
- Current availability route and a clear call to action
- Original photos and real business experience where available

### Answer format

Answer the customer's core question directly in the first 1–2 sentences. Follow with the specifics, limitations, and a clear next step. Use descriptive headings, concise paragraphs, lists, and comparison tables when they genuinely help the reader.

## Priority 3 — Build a useful FAQ resource

Candidate questions to answer once facts are confirmed:
- What equipment can I rent in Saskatoon?
- Do you rent mini excavators and skid steers?
- What projects are suitable for a mini skid steer?
- What size dump trailer do I need for my project?
- Do you offer delivery to Warman and Martensville?
- How do pickup and return work?
- Can I rent by the day, week, or month?
- What is included in the rental price?
- Do I need my own tow vehicle or hitch?
- What deposits, identification, and payment methods are required?
- How do I check availability or request a quote?
- What happens if equipment has a problem during the rental?

Keep each answer accurate and specific to the business. Do not fill missing details with assumptions.

## Priority 4 — Entity and trust signals

- [ ] Ensure consistent business name, phone, website, and legitimate address/service-area details across the website and important profiles.
- [ ] Provide a clear About page describing the actual business, local experience, fleet, and service process.
- [ ] Make contact details, rental policies, delivery information, and terms easy to find.
- [ ] Request genuine customer reviews through a compliant process; never buy or fabricate reviews.
- [ ] Use authentic photos and useful project examples with permission.
- [ ] Earn relevant local mentions from real suppliers, associations, partners, community organizations, and customers.
- [ ] Ensure all claims about equipment condition, service areas, and availability are verifiable.

## Priority 5 — Structured data

Audit before adding anything. Use only valid, supported schema that reflects visible page content:
- Organization or LocalBusiness details as appropriate
- BreadcrumbList where applicable
- Product/Offer only where the page and business model genuinely support the properties
- FAQPage only when semantically appropriate; do not promise Google FAQ rich results

Google removed FAQ rich results from Search in May 2026, so FAQ content should be created for users and answer clarity, not as a rich-result trick. Validate any markup and remove contradictions with visible page content.

## Priority 6 — Answer engine visibility testing

Run a consistent monthly prompt set in Google Search/AI features, ChatGPT, and Gemini. Record date, exact prompt, whether the business is named, citation/link, competitors mentioned, factual errors, and recommended action.

Prompt set:
1. Where can I rent a mini excavator in Saskatoon?
2. Who rents a Bobcat MT100 or mini skid steer in Saskatoon?
3. Where can I rent a dump trailer in Saskatoon?
4. Which Saskatoon rental companies offer equipment delivery to Warman?
5. What should I compare when renting a mini excavator for a backyard project?
6. Where can a contractor rent equipment for a short landscaping job near Saskatoon?
7. What should I check before renting a trailer to haul equipment?
8. Compare local options for skid steer and mini excavator rentals in Saskatoon.

Do not treat one answer as a ranking metric. AI responses vary. Look for repeatable patterns, correct citations, and whether the business's factual information is represented accurately.

## AEO success measures

- Important pages indexed and internally linked
- Search Console impressions/clicks for conversational and question-based queries
- Organic visits to equipment pages
- Qualified calls and inquiry submissions
- Booking starts and completed bookings
- Accurate business details in answer-engine responses
- Frequency and quality of citations/mentions in a fixed monthly prompt sample

## Guardrails

- No fabricated testimonials, fake authority, or artificial mentions.
- No mass-produced near-duplicate city pages.
- No unsupported "best", "cheapest", availability, delivery, or performance claims.
- No guarantee of AI citations or answer placement.
- No live website edits without explicit approval.
